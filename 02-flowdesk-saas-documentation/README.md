## Core Concepts

### Workspaces

A workspace is the central environment where a team manages its workflows, integrations, and members.

### Integrations

Integrations allow Flowdesk to connect with external applications and services.

Examples include:

* Email platforms
* CRM systems
* Messaging tools
* Spreadsheets
* Webhook endpoints

### Workflows

A workflow defines what Flowdesk should do when a specific event occurs.

A workflow typically contains:

```text
Trigger
   ↓
Condition
   ↓
Action
   ↓
Result
```

For example:

```text
New customer submits form
        ↓
Flowdesk receives trigger
        ↓
Check customer information
        ↓
Create CRM contact
        ↓
Send notification
```

## Example Automation

A business could use Flowdesk to automate lead management:

```text
Website form
     ↓
New lead submitted
     ↓
Flowdesk trigger
     ↓
Check lead information
     ↓
Create CRM contact
     ↓
Send Slack notification
     ↓
Send welcome email
```

Instead of manually performing each step, Flowdesk can execute the workflow automatically.
