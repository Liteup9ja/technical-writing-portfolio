# Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers common questions about Dangeaky Alert Vitals (DGAV).

---

## What is DGAV?

Dangeaky Alert Vitals (DGAV) is a Telegram-based trading alert bot that delivers trading alerts directly to users through Telegram direct messages.

Alerts can include information such as price, the alert trigger, conditions that were met, and additional details.

---

## How do I start using DGAV?

Open the DGAV bot in Telegram and send:

```text
/start
```

Follow the instructions provided by the bot.

See [Getting Started](/03-dgav-trading-alert-bot/getting-started) for the complete setup guide.

---

## How are DGAV alerts delivered?

DGAV delivers trading alerts through Telegram direct messages.

Make sure the DGAV bot has been started and that Telegram can receive messages from the bot.

---

## What information can an alert contain?

A DGAV alert may contain:

* Asset or market
* Price
* Alert trigger
* Conditions that were met
* Additional details

The information included may vary depending on the alert.

See [Alert Format](/03-dgav-trading-alert-bot/alert-format) for more information.

---

## What commands are available?

DGAV currently provides the following commands:

```text
/start
/feature
/alert
/subscription
/plans
```

See [Commands](/03-dgav-trading-alert-bot/commands) for details about each command.

---

## How can I check my subscription?

Use:

```text
/subscription
```

This provides access to subscription-related functionality.

---

## How can I view available plans?

Use:

```text
/plans
```

The plans and features available may change as DGAV evolves.

---

## Why am I not receiving alerts?

First, check that:

1. You have started the DGAV bot.
2. You are using the correct Telegram account.
3. Your subscription is active if required.
4. The relevant alert conditions have been triggered.
5. Telegram notifications are enabled.

See [Troubleshooting](/03-dgav-trading-alert-bot/troubleshooting) for additional steps.

---

## Can I use DGAV without Telegram?

DGAV delivers its trading alerts through Telegram direct messages, so Telegram access is required for the documented alert-delivery workflow.

---

## Does every alert contain the same information?

Not necessarily.

The information included in an alert can vary depending on the specific alert generated by DGAV.

---

## Are DGAV alerts financial advice?

No.

DGAV documentation describes the alerts as informational. An alert does not guarantee a particular trading outcome.

Users should independently evaluate information provided by the system before making trading decisions.

---

## What should I do if a command does not work?

First:

1. Confirm that the command is spelled correctly.
2. Make sure it begins with `/`.
3. Send `/start`.
4. Try the command again.
5. Review [Troubleshooting](/03-dgav-trading-alert-bot/troubleshooting).

If the issue continues, provide the DGAV administrator with details about the command and the behavior you experienced.
