# Alerts

Dangeaky Alert Vitals (DGAV) delivers trading alerts directly to users through Telegram direct messages.

An alert provides information about a triggered event and the conditions associated with that event.

---

## How Alerts Work

DGAV generates an alert when the configured conditions for an alert are triggered.

The resulting alert is delivered to the user's Telegram direct message.

An alert may contain:

* Price
* Alert trigger
* Conditions that were met
* Additional details

The information included depends on the specific alert generated by DGAV.

---

## Receiving an Alert

When DGAV generates an eligible alert, it is delivered through Telegram.

Users do not need to continuously monitor the bot conversation for a message to be generated.

Make sure Telegram notifications are enabled if you want to receive alerts promptly.

---

## Alert Information

A DGAV alert can contain several pieces of information.

### Price

The price associated with the alert when it was triggered.

### Alert Trigger

The event or signal that caused the alert to be generated.

### Conditions Met

The conditions that were satisfied when the alert was triggered.

### Additional Details

Additional information associated with the alert.

The exact fields and values may vary depending on the alert.

---

## Example Alert

The following is a simplified example of how an alert may be presented:

```text
DGAV ALERT

Asset: [Market]
Price: [Price]

Alert Trigger: [Alert Type]

Conditions Met:
- [Condition 1]
- [Condition 2]

Additional Details:
[Alert information]
```

This example is for documentation purposes. The actual alert format may differ.

---

## Understanding Alert Conditions

Alert conditions describe the requirements that were met when DGAV generated an alert.

When reviewing an alert, users should consider:

1. The price shown in the alert.
2. The trigger associated with the alert.
3. The conditions listed as met.
4. Any additional information included in the message.

---

## Alert Delivery

DGAV delivers alerts through Telegram direct messages.

If an expected alert is not received, check:

* Your Telegram connection.
* Whether the DGAV bot has been started.
* Your subscription status, if applicable.
* Whether the relevant alert conditions have been triggered.
* Your Telegram notification settings.

For additional troubleshooting, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

---

## Important Notice

DGAV alerts are informational.

Receiving an alert does not guarantee a particular trading outcome. Users should make their own decisions based on their circumstances and risk considerations.
