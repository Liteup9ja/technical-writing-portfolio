# Alert Format

This guide explains the information that may appear in a Dangeaky Alert Vitals (DGAV) trading alert.

DGAV delivers alerts through Telegram direct messages.

---

## Alert Structure

A DGAV alert may contain information about:

* The relevant market or asset
* Price
* The alert that was triggered
* Conditions that were met
* Additional details

The exact fields displayed may vary depending on the alert.

---

## Example

A simplified DGAV alert can be represented as:

```text
DGAV ALERT

Asset: [Market]

Price: [Price]

Alert Trigger: [Alert Type]

Conditions Met:
- [Condition 1]
- [Condition 2]

Additional Details:
[Alert information]
```

This is a documentation example and does not represent a specific live alert.

---

## Asset

The asset or market associated with the alert.

For example:

```text
Asset: [Market]
```

The actual asset shown depends on the alert generated by DGAV.

---

## Price

The price associated with the alert when the alert was triggered.

```text
Price: [Price]
```

The displayed value depends on the relevant market and alert.

---

## Alert Trigger

The alert trigger describes the event that caused DGAV to generate the alert.

```text
Alert Trigger: [Alert Type]
```

The trigger shown depends on the configuration of the alert.

---

## Conditions Met

This section identifies the conditions that were satisfied when the alert was generated.

```text
Conditions Met:
- [Condition 1]
- [Condition 2]
```

Multiple conditions may be included when applicable.

---

## Additional Details

Additional information may be included to provide context about the alert.

```text
Additional Details:
[Alert information]
```

The content of this section depends on the specific alert.

---

## Reading an Alert

When you receive a DGAV alert, review the information in the order presented.

A useful approach is:

1. Identify the asset or market.
2. Review the price.
3. Check the alert trigger.
4. Review the conditions that were met.
5. Read any additional details.

This helps you understand what information the alert is communicating.

---

## Alert Delivery

DGAV sends alerts through Telegram direct messages.

If you are not receiving expected alerts, first confirm that:

* You have started the DGAV bot.
* Your Telegram account is active.
* Your subscription status is valid if applicable.
* The relevant alert conditions have been triggered.
* Telegram notifications are enabled.

For additional help, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

---

## Important Notice

DGAV alerts provide information about triggered conditions.

An alert is not a guarantee of a particular trading result. Users should independently evaluate any information provided by the system before making decisions.
