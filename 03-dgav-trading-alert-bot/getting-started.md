# Getting Started

This guide explains how to start using Dangeaky Alert Vitals (DGAV) through Telegram.

DGAV delivers trading alerts directly to users through Telegram direct messages.

---

## Before You Begin

Before using DGAV, make sure:

* You have an active Telegram account.
* You can access Telegram on your device.
* You have access to the DGAV bot.
* Your DGAV account or subscription is configured if required for the features you want to use.

---

## Start the Bot

Open the DGAV bot in Telegram.

Send:

```text
/start
```

The bot will respond with the available options for your account.

Follow the instructions provided by the bot to continue.

---

## Explore DGAV

After starting the bot, you can use the available commands to interact with DGAV.

Common commands include:

| Command         | Purpose                                   |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `/start`        | Start or restart interaction with the bot |
| `/feature`      | View available features                   |
| `/alert`        | Access alert-related functionality        |
| `/subscription` | View or manage subscription information   |
| `/plans`        | View available plans                      |

The options available to you may depend on your account and the current DGAV configuration.

---

## Receiving Trading Alerts

DGAV sends triggered trading alerts directly to your Telegram DM.

An alert may contain information such as:

* Asset or market
* Price
* Alert triggered
* Conditions that were met
* Additional alert details

For example:

```text
Asset: [Market]

Price: [Price]

Alert: [Alert Type]

Conditions Met:
- [Condition 1]
- [Condition 2]

Additional Details:
[Alert information]
```

The actual information displayed depends on the alert generated by DGAV.

---

## Understanding an Alert

When you receive an alert, review the information provided by DGAV.

Pay particular attention to:

### Price

The price associated with the triggered alert.

### Alert Trigger

The event or signal that caused the alert to be generated.

### Conditions Met

The conditions that were satisfied when the alert was triggered.

### Additional Details

Other information included by DGAV to provide context for the alert.

---

## Subscription and Plans

DGAV provides subscription-related commands for users who need access to subscription information or available plans.

Use:

```text
/subscription
```

to access subscription-related functionality.

Use:

```text
/plans
```

to view available plans.

The plans and features available may change as DGAV evolves.

---

## If You Are Not Receiving Alerts

If you expect an alert but do not receive one:

1. Confirm that you have started the DGAV bot.
2. Check that you are using the correct Telegram account.
3. Review your subscription status if alerts require an active subscription.
4. Check whether the relevant alert conditions have actually been triggered.
5. Review the bot's available alert options.
6. If the issue continues, follow the troubleshooting guide.

See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for more help.
